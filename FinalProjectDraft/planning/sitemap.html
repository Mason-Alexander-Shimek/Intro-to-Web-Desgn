Vallam Lore Reference — Sitemap

Six pages, one level deep. The structure mirrors the source document: it opens on the whole world and narrows, page by page, until it reaches a single street in one city.

Site-level structure

index.html HOME · five gateways, the city map, the epigraph
world.html The World Morphe: its day and moon, climate, the Nihil Oceans, the continents, and the two religions. 6 sections.
dominion.html Fairbourne The founding, Crown and Parliament, the Dominion, and the Great War. 5 sections.
vallam.html Vallam Culture, prohibition, immigration, the city map, and 9 located neighbourhoods. 7 sections.
factions.html Factions 23 organisations in 6 categories: government, press, civic, academic, cultural, and the Congregation.
houses.html Houses The 9 families of Aldenmere, and 7 criminal organisations. 2 categories.
There is no second level, and that is deliberate. An entry for a faction, a family or a neighbourhood is an anchor inside its category page, not a page of its own. Giving 48 entries their own pages would mean 48 round trips for a reader who is usually comparing two of them. Keeping them on one page means a GM can hold the whole Congregation in a single scroll, and the sticky rail still makes any one of them reachable in a click.

How a reader moves through it

Every page reaches every other page from the main nav, so none of the three visitors below is ever more than two clicks from what they came for.

One structural change under consideration

The one component slot left under the 15 cap is reserved for a client-side filter. It would not add a page or change this map. It sits at the top of factions.html and houses.html, narrowing the entries already on the page. Findability is currently carried entirely by the rail and the two indexes, and factions.html is about 18,000 pixels tall.
