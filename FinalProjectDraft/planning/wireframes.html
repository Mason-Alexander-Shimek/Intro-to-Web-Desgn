The budget is full

Fifteen of fifteen. Any further component needs either a consolidation or a conversation. The honest consolidations available, if a slot is needed later: .crest-index and .place-index are both grids of small uniform tiles and could become one .tile-index with modifiers; .lore-list is arguably a type style rather than a component. Either would free one slot without losing anything on the page.

How Flexbox and Grid were decided

Every repeated block was measured before a component was chosen, because choosing first is the mistake the Project 1 post-mortem recorded.
