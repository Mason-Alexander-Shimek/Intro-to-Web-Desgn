Clinic with no insurance needed

Run by [Organisation name]

A regular doctor's appointment. The cost is set by what you earn and is agreed before you are seen.

Do I qualify?

  • Anyone. No insurance needed and no residency check.
  • The fee is set on a sliding scale. The lowest band is $25 a visit.
  • Nobody is turned away for being unable to pay that day. Say so at the desk.

What do I bring?

  • Photo ID if you have one. It is not required.
  • Proof of income for the last 30 days, to set your fee
  • A list of medicines you take, including the doses
  • Your insurance card, if you have one

How do I apply?

  1. Call for an appointment. Same-week slots are usually free.
  2. Arrive 15 minutes early the first time to set your fee.
  3. You are told the cost before you see the doctor, not after.
  4. Prescriptions are sent to the pharmacy of your choice.

Help paying for prescriptions

Run by [Organisation name]

Covers the cost of a medicine you have been prescribed but cannot afford this month.

Do I qualify?

  • You have a current prescription from any doctor.
  • Household of one earning $2,510 a month or less. Add $772 for each extra person.
  • Covers most regular medicines. Does not cover over-the-counter items.

What do I bring?

  • The prescription, or the empty bottle with the label
  • Photo ID
  • Proof of income for the last 30 days

How do I apply?

  1. Bring the papers to the desk. No appointment.
  2. A decision is given while you wait, usually within 30 minutes.
  3. If yes, they call the pharmacy and pay directly.
  4. Collect the medicine the same day.

Dental care

Run by [Organisation name]

Fillings, extractions, and cleaning at a set low cost. Pain is seen sooner than routine work.

Do I qualify?

  • Anyone. No insurance needed.
  • Fees are fixed and listed at the desk. An extraction is $75. A cleaning is $40.
  • A payment plan can be set up before treatment, not after.

What do I bring?

  • Photo ID if you have one
  • A list of medicines you take
  • Proof of income, if you want the lower fee band

How do I apply?

  1. Call to join the list. The wait for routine work is about 6 weeks.
  2. If you are in pain, say so. Pain is usually seen within 3 days.
  3. You are told the price before treatment starts.
  4. Pay on the day, or set up a plan at the desk.
