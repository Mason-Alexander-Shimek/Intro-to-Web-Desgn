Vallam

A lore reference for an eldritch mystery campaign set on Morphe, Anno 1926.

This is a reference for players, not a novel. It opens on the broad shape of the world and narrows, section by section, until it reaches a single street in Vallam.

Nothing here needs reading in order. Every part has its own index, every organisation its own entry, and every neighbourhood its place on the plan. Start wherever your question is.

The WorldMorphe, its moon and its oceans. Why nobody crosses the Nihil, and what lives under it. FairbourneThe Crown, Parliament, and a war that began in 1903 and has not formally ended. VallamSix and a half million people, twelve neighbourhoods, and a prohibition nobody keeps. FactionsPolice, press, clubs and the Suppression Bureau, which removes books that notice too much. HousesNine families on the hill. Seven outfits below it. Every one of them has a crest.

What is permitted for others is not permitted for you.

— motto on the badge of the Suppression Bureau
