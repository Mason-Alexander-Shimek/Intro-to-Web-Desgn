Grocery pantry

Run by [Organisation name]

A week of groceries you choose yourself, once a week. No appointment.

Do I qualify?

  • You live in Missoula County.
  • There is no income check and no paperwork for the first visit.
  • After the first visit you give a household size and a zip code. Nothing else.

What do I bring?

  • Bags or a box. The pantry runs out of both.
  • Nothing else is required.

How do I apply?

  1. Walk in during opening hours.
  2. Give your household size at the desk.
  3. You walk the aisles and choose. Staff tell you how many of each item.
  4. Come back any time after seven days.

SNAP food benefits

Run by [Organisation name]

A card topped up monthly, used like a bank card at most grocery shops. Applying is free and does not affect immigration status for most people.

Do I qualify?

  • Household of one earning $1,632 a month or less before tax.
  • Household of two earning $2,215 or less. Add $584 for each extra person.
  • Savings are not counted for most households.
  • If you are over these numbers but pay high rent or medical costs, apply anyway. Those costs are taken off.

What do I bring?

  • Photo ID
  • Social Security numbers for everyone applying
  • Last 30 days of pay stubs for everyone who works
  • Your rent or mortgage amount, and your heating bill
  • Childcare costs, if you pay any

How do I apply?

  1. Apply online, by phone, or in person. All three take the same time.
  2. An interview follows within 7 days. It is usually by phone and takes about 20 minutes.
  3. Send any papers they ask for within 10 days or the application closes.
  4. A decision comes within 30 days. If you have almost no income it can be 7 days, so say that on the interview.

Free meals for children

Run by [Organisation name]

A hot meal for anyone 18 or under. No sign-up, no names taken.

Do I qualify?

  • Anyone aged 18 or under.
  • No income check. No registration.
  • An adult may come with the child but the meal is for the child.

What do I bring?

  • Nothing.

How do I apply?

  1. Turn up during serving hours.
  2. The child eats on site. Meals cannot be taken away.
