Free and reduced bus passes

Run by [Organisation name]

A monthly pass, or single-day passes while you wait for the monthly one.

Do I qualify?

  • You live in Missoula County.
  • Household of one earning $2,006 a month or less. Add $707 for each extra person.
  • Students, people over 62, and people with a disability qualify at any income.
  • Day passes are given out with no income check at all.

What do I bring?

  • Photo ID
  • Proof of address, such as a bill or a letter
  • Proof of income for the last 30 days, for the monthly pass only

How do I apply?

  1. Walk in during opening hours. No appointment.
  2. Day passes are handed over the same visit.
  3. The monthly pass takes about a week. Ask for day passes to cover the gap.
  4. Renew the monthly pass in the last week of each month or it lapses.

Rides to medical appointments

Run by [Organisation name]

A booked ride to and from a medical appointment, for people who cannot use the bus.

Do I qualify?

  • The trip has to be to a medical appointment. Other trips are not covered.
  • You cannot drive yourself and cannot use the bus for the trip.
  • No income limit, but you must book in advance.

What do I bring?

  • Nothing for the ride itself.
  • Have your appointment date, time and the clinic address ready when you call.

How do I apply?

  1. Call at least 3 working days before the appointment. Sooner is better.
  2. Give the appointment date, time, and address.
  3. You are told your pickup time on the call. Write it down.
  4. Call the same number if the appointment moves, or the ride is lost.

Help with car repairs

Run by [Organisation name]

A one-time payment toward a repair that is stopping you getting to work.

Do I qualify?

  • You own the car and it is registered to you.
  • The repair has to be what is stopping you working or getting to medical care.
  • Household of one earning $2,510 a month or less. Add $772 for each extra person.
  • One payment per household every two years.

What do I bring?

  • Photo ID
  • The vehicle registration in your name
  • A written estimate from a garage
  • Proof of income for the last 30 days
  • Proof you are working, or a letter from your employer

How do I apply?

  1. Get a written estimate first. Applications without one are not reviewed.
  2. Call to book an appointment and bring the papers above.
  3. A decision takes about 2 weeks.
  4. If yes, payment goes straight to the garage. Do not pay for the repair first and ask to be paid back.
