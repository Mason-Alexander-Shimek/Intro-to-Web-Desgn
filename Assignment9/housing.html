Emergency rent and deposit help

Run by [Organisation name]

A one-time payment made straight to your landlord. It does not come to you.

Do I qualify?

  • You live in Missoula County.
  • Your household earns $2,430 a month or less for two people. Add $510 for each extra person.
  • You have a notice, a late fee, or a deposit you cannot pay.
  • You have not used this fund in the last 12 months.

What do I bring?

  • Photo ID for the person signing the lease
  • Your lease, or a letter from your landlord
  • The notice or bill you need help with
  • Last 30 days of pay stubs, or a benefits letter
  • Your landlord's name, phone number and mailing address

How do I apply?

  1. Call to book a 30 minute appointment. Walk-ins are not taken.
  2. Bring the papers above to the appointment.
  3. A caseworker checks your papers while you wait.
  4. You are told yes or no the same day.
  5. If yes, payment reaches your landlord in 5 to 10 working days. Ask for a copy of the letter they send.

If you have an eviction notice

Run by [Organisation name]

Free advice from someone who does this every day. Call the day you get the notice, not the week it expires.

Do I qualify?

  • Anyone renting in Missoula County.
  • There is no income limit for advice.
  • There is an income limit if you need someone to appear in court with you. Ask on the call.

What do I bring?

  • The notice, including the envelope it came in
  • Your lease
  • Any texts or letters between you and your landlord

How do I apply?

  1. Call the advice line. You do not need an appointment.
  2. Say the date on your notice first. It decides how fast things move.
  3. You get advice on the same call.
  4. If your case needs someone in court, they book that with you before you hang up.

Beds for tonight

Run by [Organisation name]

Shelter beds are given out by phone from 3pm. Arriving in person does not move you up the list.

Do I qualify?

  • Anyone 18 or over.
  • Under 18 is handled by a different service. The phone line will transfer you.
  • Couples and pets can be taken, but say so on the call.

What do I bring?

  • Nothing is required to get a bed.
  • Bring ID if you have it. It is not needed.
  • One bag per person fits in the lockers.

How do I apply?

  1. Call from 3pm. Earlier calls are not held.
  2. You are given a bed number or a place on the list.
  3. Arrive before 8pm or the bed is released.
  4. Check-in reopens at 3pm the next day. A bed one night does not hold it the next.
