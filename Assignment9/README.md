# Assignment 9
I have refined the Community Resource Access website for Missoula, including five categories pages (Housing, Food, Utilities, Health Care, and Transportation), an About page, pictures of Missoula, images delivered responsively, and components that can be used multiple times. The website design takes into account accessibility and low bandwidth on mobile phones, with crisis information, printable checklists, no account system or tracking, and no forms of data collection. Category pages continue to be less than 30 KB in size, while photographs are used only on Home and About pages, as I want to make it fast for people in need to navigate the site.

All contacts, phone numbers and resource listing are placeholders. The same applies to the five photos on the site since their licensing and authors have not been confirmed (I just found them on Wikipedia). The client would need to finalize the decision regarding the name of the website, inclusion of another category covering other living expenses and whether to expand the project by including searches for resources, donations and volunteering opportunities. It would be useful to create a system for checking and updating the data provided in the listings and to maintain up-to-date update dates.
