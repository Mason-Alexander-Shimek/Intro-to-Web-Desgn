# Design Document, Module 9 Principles

**Space.** 34px spacing of hero elements and About page text blocks ensures clear separation, and 16px card gutters ensure grouping. Listing spacing is still a problem.

**Figure/Ground.** Gradient scrim guarantees desktop hero text contrast (6.99:1). Text gets moved under the picture on mobiles where contrast is only 1.24:1. Other pictures have captions under them.

**Emphasis.** The hero takes lead on the homepage, followed by equally emphasized category links. Red color emphasizes urgency, blue colors highlight phone numbers, and About page focuses on its heading.

**Variety.** There are five visual variations—heroes, banners, cards, previews, and text blocks. Blue represents actions, red represents urgency, and gold represents restrained accents.

**Rhythm.** Consistent 3:2 ratio of preview images keeps the alignment going. Homepage shows five category links before breaking the rhythm with crisis card and About page teaser.

**Affordances.** Blue links, 44px tap targets, and big crisis buttons are immediately recognizable. There are no discoveries needed because of specific calls to action.
