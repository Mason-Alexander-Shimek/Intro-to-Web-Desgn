# Design Document, Module 7 Principles
Spacing, typography, and the four-answer layout have been utilized in the design to provide hierarchy. The blue background is used to highlight the phone number information whereas the red color and the left border is used to indicate urgency. Text has been provided that complies with the AA standard whereas the lack of images provides a more utilitarian feel to the design. Even though the white-border and the label around the “Where and when?” box improve the readability aspect of the design, unnecessary whitespace occurs due to the even height of the answers. The checklist is not relevant to the design and the “CALL FIRST” box needs extra space because of its significance. The homepage highlights the four-category links whereas the category pages highlight the urgent notifications and the phone numbers. Calls to action need prominence and the organizational credits should be shifted to the location information. Blue color indicates actions whereas the red color indicates urgency. Harmoniousness and predictability of the design have been achieved through consistent typography and listing but the twelve same panels may cause monotony on mobiles.
