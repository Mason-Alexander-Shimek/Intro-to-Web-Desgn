Help with heating bills

Run by [Organisation name]

A payment made straight to your energy company, once per winter. Applications open 1 October.

Do I qualify?

  • Household of one earning $2,510 a month or less.
  • Household of two earning $3,282 or less. Add $772 for each extra person.
  • You pay for heat, whether the bill is in your name or included in your rent.
  • You can apply whether or not you have a shut-off notice.

What do I bring?

  • Photo ID
  • Social Security numbers for everyone in the household
  • Last 30 days of income for everyone who works
  • Your most recent heating bill, or a letter from your landlord if heat is in your rent
  • Your account number with the energy company

How do I apply?

  1. Apply online or in person. Applications open 1 October and close 30 April.
  2. A decision takes about 4 weeks.
  3. If you have a shut-off notice, say so on the application. It moves to about 48 hours.
  4. The payment goes to the energy company, not to you. Your next bill shows it as a credit.

Emergency help with a shut-off

Run by [Organisation name]

A smaller payment that is decided fast, for a bill already past due.

Do I qualify?

  • You have a shut-off notice, or your service is already off.
  • Household income at or below the numbers listed for heating help above.
  • One payment per household per year.

What do I bring?

  • The shut-off notice
  • Photo ID
  • Proof of income for the last 30 days

How do I apply?

  1. Call the same day you get the notice.
  2. They call the energy company with you on the line.
  3. A decision is usually given within 48 hours.
  4. You may be asked to pay part of the bill. Ask what that amount is before you agree.

Cheaper phone and internet

Run by [Organisation name]

A monthly discount on one phone or internet bill per household.

Do I qualify?

  • You already get SNAP, Medicaid, or another listed benefit, or
  • Household of one earning $2,006 a month or less. Add $707 for each extra person.
  • One discount per household, not per person.

What do I bring?

  • Photo ID
  • Your benefit award letter, if you have one
  • Proof of income for the last 30 days, if you do not

How do I apply?

  1. Apply online, or ask at the desk for help doing it.
  2. Approval usually takes a few days.
  3. Take your approval to the phone or internet company. They apply the discount.
  4. The discount does not start until you do that last step. It is the step most often missed.
