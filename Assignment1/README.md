# A Setting for a Cyberpunk TTRPG Campaign

## A player's guide to Moscow, the Orihia Corporation, and the world outside the wall

This webpage is a lore reference for players in my cyberpunk tabletop roleplaying campaign. It covers how the world fell apart and was rebuilt by megacorporations, what the city of Moscow looks like today under the Orihia Corporation, and the companies, syndicates, and cults a character might work for, fight against, or run from.

The original lore lived in a PDF that I handed out to players. Turning it into a webpage means anyone at the table can pull it up on a phone or laptop mid-session, jump straight to the district or faction they need, and always be looking at the most current version.
